## Cross-cutting risks

1. **Reviewer access = the video.** Transparency queries remain `queryWithAuth` by decision (#332), so a reviewer cannot open the dashboard. The walkthrough video is the only way they see the three deliverables working, and it is not recorded. It is now the critical path, together with its seed data (`dispute_reversal`, a batch cover, an executed payout).
2. **Testnet reserve is in cUSD; the guarantee book is in BRL.** The vault holds a mock USD-pegged testnet asset. The page does not convert one into the other: reserve-side ratios (coverage ratio, capacity) are in cUSD, book-side figures (exposure, default rate) are in R$, and the PTAX line is marked indicative. On testnet the on-chain registry and the Convex book are also independent books. So no figure on the page says "the R$ book is covered N×" — and none should be claimed in the SOW, the video or `mutav#196`. `docs/transparency-metrics.md` says this; the narration should too.
3. **`dispute_reversal` has no UI.** Capacity release is correct and tested, but only reachable from the Convex CLI. Show it via seed, or add a staff close dialog if a reviewer wants to drive it.
4. **`apps/fund` is fully mocked** (`apps/fund/src/components/investor/transparency-page.tsx` literals) and unauthenticated. Keep it out of every evidence artifact.
